# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Decision-makers at startups and established companies (CTOs, founders, product
leads) evaluating a software partner, plus professionals and small teams looking
for a workspace at the BlackBox Coworking in Premià de Mar. They arrive
skeptical of generic agencies and want proof of craft, judgement and reliability
before they reach out.

## Product Purpose

A marketing site for SoftDevArts ("Software Development Artisans"), a software
consulting and custom-development studio. It also houses two sub-brands: AZA
(an in-house family-wellbeing product) and BlackBox Coworking (the physical
office). Success is a qualified contact: the visitor trusts the team's
craftsmanship enough to start a conversation.

## Brand Personality

Crafted, precise, confident — engineering with a maker's hand. The voice is
direct and unhyped: it shows competence rather than claiming it. Warm enough to
feel human, structured enough to feel like engineers. Three words: machined,
deliberate, opinionated.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS-template landing pages (gradient blobs, hero-metric template,
  identical icon-card grids, tiny tracked uppercase eyebrow over every section).
- Cold enterprise/consultancy stock-photo corporate look.
- Cutesy/childish interpretation of the yellow (it is a press/industrial yellow,
  not a smiley emoji yellow).

## Design Principles

- **Show the craft.** The site itself is the portfolio; every detail is the
  argument. Sloppiness anywhere undermines the whole pitch.
- **Yellow is a structural material, not a garnish.** Used as full surfaces and
  blocks carrying black ink (high contrast), not as thin decorative accents.
- **Black, white, yellow — earn each surface.** Rhythm of canvas / ink / signal;
  no surface is decorative-by-default.
- **Architectural restraint.** Strong type, generous negative space, intentional
  structure (schematic grid, hairlines). Confidence, not noise.
- **Distinct sub-brand worlds.** AZA (warm, soft) and BlackBox (dark, calm) keep
  their own register; the main brand stays press-yellow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1 (black ink on white, black ink on yellow both
pass comfortably; never yellow text on white). Visible focus rings. Full
`prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for every animation. Keyboard-operable nav
and forms.
